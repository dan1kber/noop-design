Navigation, before and after

Before · 4 tabs

Today · Trends · Sleep · More

  • More holds 28 destinations in four collapsible groups, including everyday ones: workouts, live heart rate, Breathe, Coach.
  • Rest has its own tab; Charge and Effort do not.
  • Explore and Trends are two separate ways to browse the same metrics.
  • Four unlabelled icon buttons in the Today header.
After · 5 tabs + quick actions

Today · Sleep · Activity · Trends · More

  • Activity is new: start a workout, live heart rate, training load, Breathe, Intervals, Lift log.
  • Trends absorbs Explore: one searchable list of every metric.
  • More drops to 18 rows with a description each, search, and the strap card on top.
  • Plus is always in reach for logging and starting things.
Visual system

Four rules

  • Aura squircles carry identity: warm for Charge, blue for Effort, violet for Rest. Utility lists stay plain.
  • Dot-matrix numerals (Geist Pixel) for readings only; Geist for everything you read as a sentence.
  • One lime marker means "you, now" on every scale, and nothing else is lime.
  • Tick scales replace bare sparklines so each value sits inside your typical range.
